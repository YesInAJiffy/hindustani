## ✅ **Raag Yaman**
**Thaat:** Kalyan  
**Time:** Evening (6 PM – 9 PM)  
**Vadi:** Ga  
**Samvadi:** Ni  
**Aroha:** Ni Re Ga Ma(♯) Pa Dha Ni Sa  
**Avroha:** Sa Ni Dha Pa Ma(♯) Ga Re Sa  
**Pakad:** Ni Re Ga Ma(♯) Pa, Ga Re Sa  
**Mood:** Peaceful, devotional  

**Raga Vistar:**  
Start with slow elaboration in Vilambit laya, emphasizing Ga and Ni, using meend and gamak. Gradually introduce Pa and Ma(♯).

**Bada Khayal (Sthayee & Antara):**  
- **Sthayee:** “Payoji maine Ram ratan dhan payo”  
- **Antara:** Emphasizes Ni and Sa in the upper register.

**Swara Notation Example:**  
```
Sthayee: Ni Re Ga Ma(♯) | Pa Dha Ni Sa ||
Antara: Sa Ni Dha Pa | Ma(♯) Ga Re Sa ||
```

---

## ✅ **Raag Bhairav**
**Thaat:** Bhairav  
**Time:** Morning (6 AM – 8 AM)  
**Vadi:** Dha  
**Samvadi:** Re  
**Aroha:** Sa Re(komal) Ga Ma Pa Dha(komal) Ni Sa  
**Avroha:** Sa Ni Dha(komal) Pa Ma Ga Re(komal) Sa  
**Pakad:** Sa Re(komal) Ga Ma Pa, Dha(komal) Pa Ma Ga Re(komal) Sa  
**Mood:** Serious, devotional  

**Raga Vistar:**  
Begin with slow elaboration on Re(komal) and Dha(komal), highlighting the gravity of the raga. Use oscillations on Re and Dha.

**Bada Khayal (Sthayee & Antara):**  
- **Sthayee:** “Shankar Girija Pati”  
- **Antara:** Moves to upper Sa and Ni.

**Swara Notation Example:**  
```
Sthayee: Sa Re(komal) Ga Ma | Pa Dha(komal) Ni Sa ||
Antara: Sa Ni Dha(komal) Pa | Ma Ga Re(komal) Sa ||
```

---

## ✅ **Raag Bageshri**
**Thaat:** Kafi  
**Time:** Late night (9 PM – midnight)  
**Vadi:** Ma  
**Samvadi:** Sa  
**Aroha:** Sa Ga(komal) Ma Dha Ni(komal) Sa  
**Avroha:** Sa Ni(komal) Dha Ma Ga(komal) Re Sa  
**Pakad:** Ma Dha Ni(komal) Dha Ma Ga(komal) Re Sa  
**Mood:** Romantic, serene  

**Raga Vistar:**  
Start with Ma as the focal point, use Komal Ga and Ni to create the soft, romantic feel. Include meend between Ma and Ga.

**Bada Khayal (Sthayee & Antara):**  
- **Sthayee:** “Ab mori baat suno”  
- **Antara:** Emphasizes upper Sa and Ni(komal).

**Swara Notation Example:**  
```
Sthayee: Sa Ga(komal) Ma Dha | Ni(komal) Sa ||
Antara: Sa Ni(komal) Dha Ma | Ga(komal) Re Sa ||
```

### **Q.2 Write Dhrupad with Sthayee, Antara and Tigun of Sthayee in Pt. Paluskar Notation System**

---

#### ✅ **About Dhrupad**
- **Style:** Oldest form of Hindustani classical vocal music.
- **Accompaniment:** Pakhawaj, Tanpura.
- **Taal:** Commonly Sultaal (10 matras) or Chautaal (12 matras).
- **Features:** Nom-Tom Aalap, Layakari, emphasis on purity of swar.

---

#### ✅ **Example Composition in Raag Bhairav (Dhrupad)**
**Taal:** Chautaal (12 matras)  
**Raag:** Bhairav  
**Sthayee:**  
```
Dha Re Ga Ma | Pa Dha Pa Ma | Ga Re Sa Sa ||
```
**Antara:**  
```
Sa Ni Dha Pa | Ma Ga Re Sa | Sa Sa Sa Sa ||
```

---

#### ✅ **Tigun of Sthayee in Pt. Paluskar Notation**
- Tigun means **triple speed**.
- Original Sthayee:  
```
Dha Re Ga Ma | Pa Dha Pa Ma | Ga Re Sa Sa ||
```
- Tigun notation:  
```
Dha Dha Dha Re Re Re Ga Ga Ga Ma Ma Ma | Pa Pa Pa Dha Dha Dha Pa Pa Pa Ma Ma Ma | Ga Ga Ga Re Re Re Sa Sa Sa Sa Sa Sa ||
```

### **Q.3 Brief Introduction of Talas with Theka and Layakari (Any Three)**

---

#### ✅ **1. Sultaal (Chaugun)**
- **Matras:** 10  
- **Vibhag:** 6  
- **Theka:**  
```
Dha Dha Din Ta | Kita Dha Ta | Kita Dha Ta |
```
- **Layakari:** Chaugun means **four times the original speed**. Each bol is repeated four times in notation.

---

#### ✅ **2. Tilwada (Dugun)**
- **Matras:** 16  
- **Vibhag:** 4 (4+4+4+4)  
- **Theka:**  
```
Dha Dha Dhin Ta | Kita Dha Ta | Dha Dha Dhin Ta | Kita Dha Ta |
```
- **Layakari:** Dugun means **double speed**. Each bol is written twice in Pt. Paluskar notation.

---

#### ✅ **3. Jhumra (Tigun)**
- **Matras:** 14  
- **Vibhag:** 3+4+3+4  
- **Theka:**  
```
Dhi Na | Dhi Dhi Na | Tin Na | Dhi Dhi Na |
```
- **Layakari:** Tigun means **triple speed**. Each bol is repeated three times in notation.

---

#### ✅ **4. Rupak (Chaugun)**  
- **Matras:** 7  
- **Vibhag:** 3+2+2  
- **Theka:**  
```
Tin Tin Na | Dhin Na | Dhin Na |
```
- **Layakari:** Chaugun (four times speed) notation applied.

---

#### ✅ **5. Dhamar (Tigun)**  
- **Matras:** 14  
- **Vibhag:** 5+2+3+4  
- **Theka:**  
```
Ka Dhi Ta Dhi Ta | Dha - | Ga Ti Ta | Ti Ta |
```
- **Layakari:** Tigun (triple speed) notation applied.

---

#### **1) ............... Swara's are komal in "Bhairav Thata".**  
**Answer:** **Re and Dha**  
**Explanation:**  
In **Bhairav Thaat**, **Re (Rishabh)** and **Dha (Dhaivat)** are **Komal (flat)** swars, while others are shuddha.

---

#### **2) A Raga should have at least ............... Swara's.**  
**Answer:** **5**  
**Explanation:**  
A raga must have **minimum 5 notes** in its structure (Audav Jati), and maximum 7 (Sampoorna).

---

#### **3) ............... is the "Thata" of "Hameer Raga".**  
**Answer:** **Kalyan**  
**Explanation:**  
Raag **Hameer** belongs to **Kalyan Thaat**, which includes Teevra Ma.

---

#### **4) In Raga "Bageshri" ............... are the "Varjit" Swara's in Aroha.**  
**Answer:** **Re and Pa**  
**Explanation:**  
In **Bageshri**, **Re and Pa** are omitted (Varjit) in Aroha, making it an Audav-Sampoorna type.

---

#### **5) ............... is the Accompanying instrument of Indian Music.**  
**Answer:** **Tanpura**  
**Explanation:**  
**Tanpura** provides the drone and is considered the main accompanying instrument in Indian classical music.

---

#### **6) There should be ............... Swara's in a "Thata".**  
**Answer:** **7**  
**Explanation:**  
A **Thaat** is a parent scale and must have **7 notes** (Sampoorna).

---

#### **7) ............... and ............... are the "Achala" Swara's.**  
**Answer:** **Sa and Pa**  
**Explanation:**  
**Sa (Shadja)** and **Pa (Pancham)** are fixed notes (Achala swars) and never change their position.

### **Q.5 Write short notes**

---

#### ✅ **1) Dividing 22 Shrutis in Seven Swaras (Modern point of view)**  
- Indian classical music recognizes **22 Shrutis** (microtones) in an octave.  
- These are distributed among **7 main swaras**:  
  - Sa (4), Re (3), Ga (2), Ma (4), Pa (4), Dha (3), Ni (2).  
- Modern view simplifies this into **12 semitones** for practical tuning, but the concept of Shrutis remains important for ornamentation and intonation.

---

#### ✅ **2) Sandhiprakash Raga**  
- **Meaning:** Ragas sung during the transition periods of day (dawn and dusk).  
- **Examples:** Raag Marwa, Raag Puriya, Raag Sohini.  
- **Features:** Use of Komal and Teevra swars, creating a serious and meditative mood.  
- **Time:** 4th and 8th Prahar (sunrise and sunset).

---

#### ✅ **3) Raga Vibhajan according to Swara Sankhya (Jaati)**  
- **Jaati** classification is based on the number of notes in Aaroh and Avaroh:  
  - **Audav:** 5 notes  
  - **Shadav:** 6 notes  
  - **Sampoorna:** 7 notes  
- Example:  
  - Bhoopali (Audav-Audav)  
  - Bihag (Shadav-Sampoorna)  
  - Yaman (Sampoorna-Sampoorna).

---

#### ✅ **4) Producing of Dhwani, Kampana also Aandolan**  
- **Dhwani:** Sound produced by vibration.  
- **Kampana:** Oscillation of a note for expressiveness.  
- **Aandolan:** Slow oscillation around a note, used in ragas like Darbari for depth.

---

#### ✅ **5) Tana with Shudha, Mishra, Sapat Variations**  
- **Tana:** Fast melodic patterns used in Khayal singing.  
- **Types:**  
  - **Shuddha Tana:** Uses only raga notes.  
  - **Mishra Tana:** Includes foreign notes or phrases.  
  - **Sapat Tana:** Straight ascending or descending sequences.  
- Example: In Raag Yaman, a sapat tana: Ni Re Ga Ma(♯) Pa Dha Ni Sa.

### **Q.6 Brief discussion on Pt. Bhatkhande and Pt. Paluskar Notation System with comparative aspects**

---

#### ✅ **Pt. Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande**
- **Contribution:**  
  - Classified ragas into **10 Thaats** (Bilawal, Kalyan, Khamaj, Bhairav, Bhairavi, Asavari, Kafi, Todi, Marwa, Poorvi).  
  - Wrote **Kramik Pustak Malika**, a series of books on Hindustani music theory.  
- **Notation System:**  
  - Uses **Roman script** for swaras (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni).  
  - Komal swar indicated by **underline (-)**, Teevra Ma by **vertical line (|)**.  
  - Taal divisions shown with **vertical bars**.  
  - Example: `S R G M | P D N S ||`

---

#### ✅ **Pt. Vishnu Digambar Paluskar**
- **Contribution:**  
  - Founded **Gandharva Mahavidyalaya** in 1901, institutionalizing music education.  
  - Popularized **Bhajans** and devotional music.  
- **Notation System:**  
  - Uses **Devanagari script** for swaras.  
  - Komal swar indicated by **dot below**, Teevra Ma by **dot above**.  
  - Taal divisions shown with **symbols like 0 for khaali and + for taali**.  
  - Example: `सा रे ग म | प ध नि सा ||`

---

#### ✅ **Comparative Aspects**
| Aspect                | Bhatkhande System           | Paluskar System            |
|----------------------|----------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Script**          | Roman                     | Devanagari               |
| **Komal Indication**| Underline (-)             | Dot below                |
| **Teevra Indication**| Vertical line (|)         | Dot above                |
| **Taal Symbols**    | Bars for divisions        | + for Taali, 0 for Khaali|
| **Usage**           | Widely used in theory     | Popular in Gandharva Mahavidyalaya |

Here’s a detailed answer for **“Write information about Khayal”**:

---

### ✅ **Khayal – Overview**
- **Meaning:** The word *Khayal* means “imagination” in Persian. It is the most popular form of Hindustani classical vocal music today.
- **Origin:** Developed during the Mughal period, influenced by Persian and Indian traditions.
- **Style:** Highly improvisational, allowing the artist to explore creativity within the framework of a raga.

---

### ✅ **Features of Khayal**
1. **Structure:**  
   - **Vilambit Khayal:** Slow tempo, used for detailed raga development.  
   - **Drut Khayal:** Fast tempo, lively and rhythmic.  
2. **Accompaniment:**  
   - Tabla for rhythm, Harmonium or Sarangi for melodic support, Tanpura for drone.  
3. **Lyrics:**  
   - Short, poetic, often devotional or romantic in nature.  
4. **Improvisation:**  
   - Includes **Alaap**, **Bol Aalap**, **Bol Taan**, and **Sargam Taan**.  
5. **Taal:**  
   - Commonly Teentaal (16 matras), Ektaal (12 matras), or Tilwada (16 matras).

---

### ✅ **Importance**
- Khayal emphasizes **freedom of expression**, making it distinct from older forms like Dhrupad.
- It showcases **voice modulation, creativity, and mastery of laya (tempo)**.
