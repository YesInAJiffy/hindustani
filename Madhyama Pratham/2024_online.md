### **Q.1 Fill in the blanks**

---

#### **1. There are ............ Thaats classified by Pt. Bhatkhande.**
**Answer:** **C) 10**  
**Explanation:** Pt. Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande classified Hindustani ragas into **10 Thaats**: Bilawal, Kalyan, Khamaj, Bhairav, Bhairavi, Asavari, Kaafi, Todi, Marwa, and Poorvi. This system is widely used in Hindustani classical music.

---

#### **2. ............ Raag has Ga and Ni Komal swar.**
**Answer:** **D) Bhairav**  
**Explanation:** Raag Bhairav belongs to the Bhairav Thaat and uses **Komal Re and Komal Dha**, but the question asks for Ga and Ni Komal. Actually, this matches **Raag Bhimpalasi** (Komal Ga and Komal Ni). So the correct answer is **B) Bhimpalasi**.  
**Correction:** Bhimpalasi is an afternoon raag from Kaafi Thaat.

---

#### **3. ............ is a Shadav-Sampoorna jaati raag in which 'Gandhar' swar is varjit in aroha.**
**Answer:** **C) Bihag**  
**Explanation:** Raag Bihag has **6 notes in Aroha (Shadav)** and **7 notes in Avroha (Sampoorna)**. Gandhar (Ga) is omitted in Aroha, making it Shadav-Sampoorna.

---

#### **4. ............ is an Odav jaati raag in which Re-Pa swar are varjit.**
**Answer:** **B) Durga**  
**Explanation:** Raag Durga is an **Audav (Pentatonic)** raag. Its notes are Sa, Ga, Ma, Dha, Ni. Re and Pa are omitted.

---

#### **5. There are ............ types of instrumental categories.**
**Answer:** **B) 4**  
**Explanation:** Instruments in Indian classical music are classified into **4 categories**:  
- **Tat Vadya** (String instruments)  
- **Avanaddha Vadya** (Percussion instruments)  
- **Sushir Vadya** (Wind instruments)  
- **Ghan Vadya** (Solid instruments like cymbals).

---

#### **6. ............ is the symbol of Khaali in Pt. Paluskar notation system.**
**Answer:** **C) 0**  
**Explanation:** In Pt. Paluskar's notation system, **0** denotes **Khaali (empty beat)** in a taal.

---

#### **7. ............ is an Ashray Raag.**
**Answer:** **B) Khamaj**  
**Explanation:** An **Ashray Raag** is the main raag of a Thaat. For example, Khamaj Thaat's Ashray Raag is **Khamaj**.

---

#### **8. The Bandish of a Raag in which the characteristics of the raag are written is known as ............**
**Answer:** **C) Lakshangeet**  
**Explanation:** **Lakshangeet** is a composition that describes the **lakshan (characteristics)** of a raag, including its notes, time of singing, and mood.

### **Q.1 Fill in the blanks (continued)**

---

#### **9. There are ............ Shrutis in an octave.**  
**Answer:** **D) 22**  
**Explanation:** In Indian classical music, an octave (Saptak) is divided into **22 Shrutis** (microtones). These are the smallest pitch intervals recognized in the system.

---

#### **10. ............ is a Uttarang Pradhan raag in which all swars are shuddha.**  
**Answer:** **D) Alhaiya Bilawal**  
**Explanation:** Raag **Alhaiya Bilawal** is a morning raag, all its notes are **shuddha (natural)**, and it emphasizes the **Uttarang (upper tetrachord)** of the scale.

---

#### **11. 'Kut' is a type of ............**  
**Answer:** **C) Taan**  
**Explanation:** In Hindustani music, **Kut** refers to a type of **taan**, which is a fast melodic phrase used in improvisation.

---

#### **12. Pt. Pannalal Ghosh died in the year ............**  
**Answer:** **B) 1960**  
**Explanation:** Pt. Pannalal Ghosh, a pioneer of the flute in Hindustani classical music, passed away in **1960**.

---

#### **13. Pt. "Ichalkaranjikar" was guru of ............**  
**Answer:** **D) Pt. Paluskar**  
**Explanation:** Pt. Balkrishnabuwa Ichalkaranjikar was the guru of **Pt. Vishnu Digambar Paluskar**, who founded Gandharva Mahavidyalaya.

---

#### **14. Birth place of Pt. Ichalkaranjikar was ............**  
**Answer:** **D) Bedag**  
**Explanation:** Pt. Ichalkaranjikar was born in **Bedag**, a village in Maharashtra.

---

#### **15. In Pt. Bhatkhande notation system the sign for komal swar is ............**  
**Answer:** **B) -**  
**Explanation:** In Bhatkhande notation, a **dash (-)** below the note indicates that the swar is **komal (flat)**.

---

#### **16. ............ is the speciality of Dhrupad singing.**  
**Answer:** **D) Layakari**  
**Explanation:** Dhrupad is known for its **Layakari**, which is the intricate play of rhythm and tempo within the taal structure.

---

#### **17. ............ was named as Ramtanu Pandey (Tanna Mishra).**  
**Answer:** **C) Tansen**  
**Explanation:** The legendary musician **Tansen** was originally named **Ramtanu Pandey**, later known as Tanna Mishra.

---

#### **18. 'Chaugun' is the speciality of ............ form of gayan.**  
**Answer:** **A) Dhrupad**  
**Explanation:** In Dhrupad singing, **Chaugun** refers to singing at four times the original tempo, a hallmark of this style.

---

#### **19. Name of 14 matra Taal is ............**  
**Answer:** **C) Dhamar**  
**Explanation:** **Dhamar Taal** has **14 matras** and is commonly used in Dhrupad and Dhamar compositions.

---

#### **20. In an octave (saptak) there are ............ vikrit swars.**  
**Answer:** **B) 5**  
**Explanation:** There are **5 vikrit (altered)** swars: Re, Ga, Dha, Ni (Komal) and Ma (Teevra).

---

#### **21. Pakhawaj is a ............ instrument.**  
**Answer:** **D) Percussion**  
**Explanation:** **Pakhawaj** is an **Avanaddha Vadya**, a percussion instrument used in Dhrupad.

---

#### **22. In Jhumra Taal there are ............ matras.**  
**Answer:** **A) 14**  
**Explanation:** **Jhumra Taal** also has **14 matras**, but its structure and theka differ from Dhamar.

### **Q.2 Multiple Choice Questions**

---

#### **1. Jaati of a Raag which has got 6 swars in Aaroh and 5 swars in Avaroh?**  
**Answer:** **D) Shadav-Audav**  
**Explanation:**  
- **Jaati** refers to the number of notes in Aaroh (ascending) and Avaroh (descending).  
- **Shadav** = 6 notes, **Audav** = 5 notes.  
- So, if Aaroh has 6 and Avaroh has 5, the jaati is **Shadav-Audav**.

---

#### **2. Pt. Shiv Kumar Sharma was famous for playing which instrument?**  
**Answer:** **D) Santoor**  
**Explanation:**  
Pt. Shiv Kumar Sharma introduced **Santoor**, a Kashmiri instrument, into Hindustani classical music and made it globally popular.

---

#### **3. Masit Khani Gat is played in which laya?**  
**Answer:** **B) Vilambit**  
**Explanation:**  
Masit Khani Gat is a slow-tempo composition in **Vilambit laya**, typically played on instruments like sitar in **Raga Yaman** or similar.

---

#### **4. In which raag Ga, Dha, Ni swars are komal?**  
**Answer:** **B) Kalingada**  
**Explanation:**  
Raag **Kalingada** uses **Komal Ga, Komal Dha, and Komal Ni**, giving it a serious and grave mood.

---

#### **5. Re, Dha swars are komal in which Raag?**  
**Answer:** **B) Jaunpuri**  
**Explanation:**  
Raag **Jaunpuri** belongs to Asavari Thaat and uses **Komal Re and Komal Dha**, making it a Sandhi Prakash raag (evening time).

### **Q.2 Multiple Choice Questions (continued)**

---

#### **6. How many Vibhag (divisions) are there in 'Sultal'?**  
**Answer:** **B) 6**  
**Explanation:**  
- **Sultal** is a 10-matra taal used in Dhrupad.  
- It is divided into **6 Vibhags** (sections), which organize the beats for rhythmic clarity.

---

#### **7. Which taal has taali on fifth matra and khaali on third matra?**  
**Answer:** **D) Teentaal**  
**Explanation:**  
- **Teentaal** has 16 matras and 4 Vibhags.  
- **Taali** (clap) is on 1st, 5th, and 13th matras, and **Khaali** (wave) is on the 9th matra.  
- The question seems tricky because khaali on 3rd matra is not typical for Teentaal, but among the options, Teentaal is the closest standard taal.

---

#### **8. In Adhunik (new) shruti vibhajan (division) 'Sa' is placed on which shruti?**  
**Answer:** **A) 4**  
**Explanation:**  
- In the modern division of 22 shrutis, **Sa** is placed on the **4th shruti**.

---

#### **9. Pt. Venkatmakhi created how many Mel/Thaats?**  
**Answer:** **A) 72**  
**Explanation:**  
- Pt. Venkatamakhi, a Carnatic musicologist, classified ragas into **72 Melakarta scales**, which are equivalent to Thaats in Hindustani music.

---

#### **10. Which instrument is known as wind (sushir) vadya?**  
**Answer:** **D) By air or Blowing**  
**Explanation:**  
- **Sushir Vadya** refers to wind instruments like flute, shehnai, harmonium, which produce sound by blowing air.

---

#### **11. "Ghaseet" word is used in what?**  
**Answer:** **B) Instrumental**  
**Explanation:**  
- **Ghaseet** means sliding or gliding, commonly used in **instrumental music** for string instruments like sitar or sarangi.

---

#### **12. Pt. Pannalal Ghosh received training in music from ............**  
**Answer:** **C) Ust. Allauddin Khan**  
**Explanation:**  
- Pt. Pannalal Ghosh, the pioneer of flute in Hindustani music, was trained by **Ustad Allauddin Khan** of Maihar Gharana.

---

#### **13. In an octave (Saptak) how many swar's are there?**  
**Answer:** **A) 7**  
**Explanation:**  
- The basic scale (Saptak) consists of **7 swars**: Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni.

---

#### **14. Pancham (Pa) swar is also known as?**  
**Answer:** **B) Achal swar**  
**Explanation:**  
- **Achal swars** are fixed notes that never change their position. Sa and Pa are achal swars.

---

#### **15. What is the differentiating feature between 'Chautal' and 'Ektaal'?**  
**Answer:** **B) Vibhag (division)**  
**Explanation:**  
- Both Chautal and Ektaal have 12 matras, but their **Vibhag structure** differs:  
  - **Chautal:** 2+2+2+2+2+2  
  - **Ektaal:** 2+2+2+2+2+2 (same matras but different bols and emphasis).
